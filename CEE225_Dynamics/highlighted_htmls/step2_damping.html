Step 2: Damping Analysis

Estimate damping ratios using logarithmic decrement method from free vibration decay

Methodology

The damping analysis uses the logarithmic decrement method on the free vibration decay portion:

  1. Decay Detection: Automatically identifies when forced vibration ends and free decay begins by detecting consistent negative slope in the amplitude envelope.
  2. Peak Extraction: Finds local maxima and minima in the decay portion using prominence and distance constraints based on the natural period.
  3. Logarithmic Decrement: Computes $\\delta = \\frac{1}{n}\\ln\\left(\\frac{x_0}{x_n}\\right)$ averaged over $n$ cycles for stability.
  4. Damping Ratio: Calculates $\\zeta = \\frac{\\delta}{\\sqrt{4\\pi^2 + \\delta^2}} \\approx \\frac{\\delta}{2\\pi}$ for small damping.
  5. Multi-Floor Analysis: Estimates damping from each floor's modal acceleration to assess consistency and compute mean ± standard deviation.
