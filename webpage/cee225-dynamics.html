Homework #2 – Key Highlights

Selected results from my solution to Homework #2 for CEE 225 (Dynamics of Structures) taught by Prof. M. DeJong.

  • Problem 1: Mass suddenly released from stops; free vibration about static equilibrium. Solution: u(t) = -(3mg/4k) cos(\sqrt{k/m} \; t). Amplitude = 3mg/4k, frequency = \sqrt{k/m}.
  • Problem 2: Inelastic impact (bullet in block) followed by SDOF free vibration with M = m + m_b. Initial velocity \dot u(0) = m_b v_b / (m + m_b). With the given data: u(t) = 0.247\,\sin(47.67 t) [in].
  • Problem 3: Vehicle suspension: stiffness from static deflection; damping from nominal \zeta'; update to \zeta with added passenger mass. Result: k = 1666.7 lb/in, \omega_n \approx 12.52 rad/s, \zeta \approx 0.647.
  • Problem 4: Bridge experiment (Davis–Cory walkway). From measured decaying peaks, damping via logarithmic decrement \delta = (1/(2\pi j))\ln(u_i/u_{i+j}). Result: \zeta \approx 1.8%, in line with literature for pedestrian bridges.

References: Chopra (2014); DeJong et al. (2025); Staacks et al. (2018); Castellanos‑Toro et al. (2018).
