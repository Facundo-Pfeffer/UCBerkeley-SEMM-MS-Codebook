Homework #2 – Key Highlights

Selected results from my solution to Homework #2 for CEE 225 (Dynamics of Structures) taught by Prof. M. DeJong.

  • Problem 1: Mass suddenly released from stops; free vibration about static equilibrium. Solution: $u(t) = -\frac{3mg}{4k} \cos\left(\sqrt{\frac{k}{m}} \, t\right)$. Amplitude = $\frac{3mg}{4k}$, frequency = $\sqrt{k/m}$.
  • Problem 2: Inelastic impact (bullet in block) followed by SDOF free vibration with $M = m + m_b$. Initial velocity $\dot{u}(0) = \frac{m_b v_b}{m + m_b}$. With the given data: $u(t) = 0.247\,\sin(47.67 \, t)$ [in].
  • Problem 3: Vehicle suspension: stiffness from static deflection; damping from nominal $\zeta'$; update to $\zeta$ with added passenger mass. Result: $k = 1666.7$ lb/in, $\omega_n \approx 12.52$ rad/s, $\zeta \approx 0.647$.
  • Problem 4: Bridge experiment (Davis–Cory walkway). From measured decaying peaks, damping via logarithmic decrement $\delta = \frac{1}{2\pi j}\ln\left(\frac{u_i}{u_{i+j}}\right)$. Result: $\zeta \approx 1.8\%$, in line with literature for pedestrian bridges.

References: Chopra (2014); DeJong et al. (2025); Staacks et al. (2018); Castellanos‑Toro et al. (2018).
