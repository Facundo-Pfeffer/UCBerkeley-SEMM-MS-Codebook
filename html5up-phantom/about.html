About Me

Academic Background

I am a graduate student in the Structural Engineering, Mechanics and Materials (SEMM) program at the University of California, Berkeley. I develop research-grade software and methods for structural dynamics, nonlinear analysis, and earthquake engineering, combining rigorous mechanics with modern scientific computing.

Research Interests

  • Structural Dynamics: Single and multi-degree of freedom systems, response spectrum analysis
  • Numerical Methods: Integration schemes, stability analysis, error propagation
  • Earthquake Engineering: Seismic response analysis, ground motion characterization
  • Computational Mechanics: Finite element methods, material modeling
  • Interactive Visualization: Scientific computing, data visualization, educational tools
